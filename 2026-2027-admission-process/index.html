OIA Admissions Process

 

Registration for the 2026-2027 school year at Ohio Ihsan Academy is open

***UPDATE: We are waitlisting for incoming Kindergarten students.

  1. Please fill out the Admission Application form here ($150)
    1. Please Note: If your child has an IEP or  504 Plan and you are interested in enrollment, please email Admin@oiacademy.org
    2. Please Note: At this time, our school will not be able to provide EL service.
    3. Please Note: Children registering for Pre-K to Kindergarten must be fully potty trained and able to use the bathroom independently.

 

  1. Ohio Ihsan Academy will email you a calendar link to arrange for an in-person interview for both parents & students.

 

  1. In-person Parent & Student Interview  

 

  1. Complete the Registration Form
      • Once a student is accepted for admission, a registration link will be sent to complete the process. The registration fee for all students is $1,050, and this fee is non-refundable.

    We are proud to participate in the EdChoice Scholarship Program. Families can apply for the EdChoice Scholarship to help cover tuition costs. For more information, please click here.

    Note: EdChoice Coverage

    The EdChoice Scholarship program provides partial tuition coverage, with the percentage varying based on family income and residential area. Families must apply to determine their eligibility and the specific coverage amount they qualify for. Please refer to the EdChoice application process for more details.

    Ohio Ihsan Academy applies EdChoice awards according to school-specific tuition and billing policies; details on how EdChoice is applied at OIA can be found on our EdChoice Information Page. 

     

     

    • Sibling Discount:
      $500 off the second child and each additional child.
      This discount is applied to the total fees.

    • Payment Plan Incentives:

      Plan A – Full Payment Incentive:
      $350 discount per student
      To qualify, full payment must be made on or before June 15.

      Plan B – Split Payment Incentive:
      $200 discount per student
      To qualify, the first half must be paid on or before June 15.

    Please Note:
    All discounts—including sibling and payment plan incentives—are applied only to the fees, not to tuition.

       

      Withdrawal Policy

      • If a child is withdrawn after June 15th, families are responsible for all program fees for the academic year
      • If a child is withdrawn after July 15th, families are responsible for the full tuition and program fees for the academic year, regardless of the withdrawal date or any changes to EdChoice payments.
      • Full tuition must be paid regardless of the status or availability of EdChoice.
      • All program fees and tuition paid are non-refundable, including the $1,050 registration fee, which secures a child’s enrollment at Ohio Ihsan Academy.
      • Sibling discount is applied exclusively to Program fees and not to tuition.
