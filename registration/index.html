Ohio Ihsan Academy School Policies

By enrolling your child at Ohio Ihsan Academy, you agree to adhere to and support the following school policies. For detailed information, please refer to the student handbook. Please read each policy summary carefully and indicate your acknowledgment and agreement.

  1. Attendance Policy
  • Regular attendance is crucial for academic success. Parents/guardians must ensure that their child attends school punctually and regularly. Absences must be reported to the school office in advance or on the day of absence. (See Student Handbook for details.)
  1. Dress Code and Uniform Policy
  • All students are required to wear the official school uniform. The uniform promotes a sense of belonging and equality. The school uniform is designed to be modest, with clothing that is below the knee for both boys and girls. (See Student Handbook for details.)
  1. Code of Conduct
  • Ohio Ihsan Academy is committed to providing a safe and respectful learning environment. Students are expected to behave respectfully towards peers, teachers, and staff, and to adhere to the school's code of conduct. Bullying, harassment, and any form of discrimination are strictly prohibited. (See Student Handbook for details.)
  1. Academic Integrity Policy
  • Academic honesty is essential. Cheating, plagiarism, and other forms of academic dishonesty are not tolerated. Students are expected to submit original work and properly attribute sources. (See Student Handbook for details.)
  1. Technology and Screen Time Policy
  • In alignment with our philosophy of holistic development and focusing on students' overall health, we believe in limiting children's use of screen time during school hours and ask that parents agree to do the same at home. We seek parental cooperation in strictly enforcing this policy. (See Student Handbook for details.)
  1. Health and Safety Policy
  • The health and safety of our students are of utmost importance. Parents/guardians must provide up-to-date health records and emergency contact information. The school will follow necessary protocols to ensure the well-being of all students. (See Student Handbook for details.)
  1. Parental Involvement and Communication Policy
  • Parental involvement is crucial for student success. We highly encourage at least one parent, ideally both, to attend school events, meetings, and conferences. This participation fosters community and supports your child's educational journey. It is essential for parents/guardians to stay informed about school updates, announcements, and their child's progress through the school's communication channels. Your engagement is greatly appreciated and vital for creating a thriving educational environment for all students. (Please refer to the Student Handbook for further details.)
  1. Consent for Participation in Activities
  • Students may participate in various school activities, including field trips and extracurricular programs. Separate consent forms will be provided for specific activities, but general acknowledgment and agreement are required for participation. (See Student Handbook for details.)
