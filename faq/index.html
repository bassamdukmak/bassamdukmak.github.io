Frequently Asked Questions

As the Muslim community in Columbus continues to grow and flourish, the demand for a high-caliber Islamic education has become more pressing. Together, fueled by passion and determination, we’ve collectively contributed our time, finances, and energy to establish an innovative new Islamic school. This school is a testament to our shared commitment to meet the growing versatile needs of our community. Join us on this exhilarating journey!

Admissions and Tuitions

 

 

  1. What is the admissions process?
          • The admissions process is outlined here.
  2. Is there an entrance exam or assessment?
          • Yes, there is an entrance process that includes an interview for students. During the interview, we conduct informal assessments to understand each child’s abilities and learning style. These assessments help us determine if we are able to meet the student’s needs in our school and ensure that we can provide the appropriate support and resources to help every student succeed.
  3. Do you accept EdChoice?
          • We are proud to participate in the EdChoice Scholarship Program. Families can apply for the EdChoice Scholarship to help cover tuition costs.
  4. Which grades do you offer?
          • In the school year 2024-25, Ohio Ihsan Academy will welcome Pre-K through third-grade students. We’ll then add one grade level each year. 
  5. What are the age requirements for the Pre-K 3 Montessori classroom?
          • To be eligible for our Pre-K 3 Montessori classroom, students must be 3 years old by September 30th.
Parents' Involvement

 

 

  • How can parents get involved in the school community?
        • Parents can get involved in the school community in several meaningful ways. Our school will have an active Parent-Teacher Organization (PTO), which is a fantastic avenue for parents to participate. Through the PTO, parents can:
              • Volunteer for Events and Activities: Help organize and run school events, fundraisers, and special programs.
              • Attend PTO Meetings: Join regular meetings to discuss upcoming events, share ideas, and collaborate with teachers and administrators.
              • Join Committees: Participate in various committees focused on specific areas like fundraising, community outreach, and school improvements.
              • Communication and Feedback: Provide valuable input and feedback on school policies and initiatives, ensuring that the parents’ voice is heard in the school community.

              We encouage all parents to get involved through the PTO to help enrich our school environment and build a strong, positive, and supportive community for our students

 

Extracurricular Activities

 

  1. What extracurricular activities are available?
          • We are excited to share that we have a dedicated committee working on organizing various after-school club enrichment opportunities. These clubs will provide students with a range of activities to explore their interests and develop new skills outside the regular classroom setting. Stay tuned for more details as we finalize the offerings!
  2. Do you offer before and after school care or programs?
          • We are currently exploring options and will provide more information as soon as possible.
Logistics

 

  1. What transportation options are available?
          • For the first year, parents will need to drop off and pick up their students. We are exploring additional transportation options for the future and will keep parents updated as new solutions become available.
  2. What are your lunch and snack policies? Do you provide meals?
          • Parents will be responsible for packing healthy lunches and snacks for their children. However, we are actively working with a team of nutritionists to introduce weekly or monthly hot lunch meals for purchase. We will keep parents informed as we finalize these plans.
  3. Where is the school located?
          • Ohio Ihsan Academy will be leasing the entire ground floor of the Noor Islamic Cultural Center building for the first year. This space will be exclusively dedicated to Ohio Ihsan Academy. The entire space will undergo renovation to create a fresh and welcoming environment for our OIA families. We’re committed to ensuring that our students have a beautiful, comfortable, and inviting place to learn and grow.
          • Following the inaugural year, Ohio Ihsan Academy will relocate to the new NOOR Ohio building. During this year, the NOOR Ohio space will undergo renovations to be transformed into a school facility.

 

     

    Montessori Program (3-5 yrs)

     

    1. What is Montessori?
            • Montessori education caters to children’s needs from early years to adolescence, fostering their skills and knowledge for success in today’s world. Teachers closely monitor children’s progress and growth through continual assessment, observing their understanding and development using learning materials and record-keeping.
            • In the Montessori primary environment, children engage in natural social interactions, aiding their social-emotional growth. Emphasis on spoken language and foundational literacy skills enhances communication abilities. Teachers observe and support each child’s unique journey, facilitated by multi-aged classrooms exposing children to diverse social roles. Moreover, students will be exposed to both English and Arabic languages, alongside Quranic studies, enriching their linguistic and cultural understanding.
