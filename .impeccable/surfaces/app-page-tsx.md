## Scope and mode

Homepage and embedded player-evaluation flow. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

The primary visitor is a serious international footballer aged 18–25, often accompanied by a parent or adviser. They need to see that Phoenix starts in a real Dubai club environment, understand the football-and-education model, discover the Manchester and Algarve routes, and know who they will learn from. The primary action is "Request a player evaluation." Partnership enquiries remain a separate secondary route.

## Proof and content

Use the supplied Phoenix Overview and local Phoenix photography. State the journey in order: Dubai / Phoenix United, Manchester / Radcliffe FC, Algarve / Silves FC. Show the named founders, ambassador, coaching, recruitment, and performance experience already approved in the product copy. Keep clear that playing time, placements, contracts, and scholarships are assessed or earned, never guaranteed.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Direction: **The Route Room** (direction seed `0934a2e3`). Combine club-tunnel wayfinding with an international departure board. The memorable moment is a visible gold line that starts at `DXB 01` in the hero and becomes the Dubai → Manchester → Algarve pathway. Photography intersects that route so the page feels inhabited rather than diagrammatic.

The refined composition keeps the route-board architecture and human tunnel scene, but stops performing the system in every section. The page now moves through distinct visual roles: asymmetric human hero, restrained bone programme, cinematic full-width squad photograph, dense route board, structured team ledger, and dark application. Gold is restricted to the active route, selected decisions, and primary actions.

## Constraints

The site must work on mobile, preserve the black/navy/bone/gold identity, use real supplied imagery, and never show success unless the lead is saved. Mobile must open as identity → headline → human image → primary action → compact route strip, with a full-screen opaque tunnel menu and no functional label below 11px. Local logos must render without an image transformation dependency. Capture attribution, selected route, and player-versus-partner intent. Keep durable local lead storage and CRM sync behavior unchanged.

## Unresolved decisions

CRM webhook URL, authentication scheme, canonical CRM field names, assignment rules, evaluation-calendar URL, WhatsApp number, final legal review, and any future staff or partner changes still require Phoenix confirmation.
