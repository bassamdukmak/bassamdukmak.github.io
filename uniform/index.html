OIA Uniform Shopping Guide

Welcome families! To make your uniform shopping experience smooth and hassle-free, we have compiled a step-by-step guide and useful information for purchasing Ohio Ihsan Academy uniforms.

Dress Code Requirements

All students are required to come dressed in the school-approved uniform. 

Uniforms

Educational Apparel, located in Hilliard, Ohio, is the official vendor for school uniforms for OIA. Families may purchase uniforms from different vendors as long as the items are an exact match to the specified requirements.

  • Girl Uniforms

Pre-K to 3rd Grade: Jumper, plain white shirt (long sleeve or short sleeve), and navy or white tights/leggings.

4th–5th Grade: Jumper, long sleeve white shirt, and navy leggings.

Students in first grade and up should bring a cover for prayer. Outside of that, if a student chooses to wear a hijab, the only allowed color is white.

  • Boy Uniforms

Pre-K–5th Grade: Khaki pants (business-casual) and solid green polo shirts (short sleeve or long sleeve).

  • Gym Uniforms

OIA PE Shirts, available for purchase directly from the school, can be worn on designated PE days. 

To buy gym uniforms, go to this link: Purchase Gym Uniforms (opens externally).

  • Additional Guidelines

Any sweater, cardigan, pullover, or zip-up jacket worn by students during school hours over their uniform for warmth must be solid navy blue or OIA green and may only display the OIA logo. No other branding, graphics, writing, or logos are permitted on clothing worn over the uniform during the school day.

Students must also bring clean shoes for classroom use only. Shoes must cover the toes; slides are not allowed. Outdoor shoes are not permitted in the classrooms.

If a student arrives on school campus out of uniform, they will be provided a uniform item to change into, and parents will be charged for the price of that uniform item.

Educational Apparel

Educational Apparel is a family business located in Columbus, Ohio, established in 1985. They serve the uniform needs of schools and parents throughout Ohio, providing top-quality brand name uniforms. Their friendly staff is available to assist you with finding the right items in the right size and answering any questions about your school’s uniform policy. Their goal is to make your shopping experience as easy and hassle-free as possible.

Store Features

– Fitting rooms
– Year-round stocked merchandise
– Hundreds of sizes to fit all children
– Finest and most durable fabrics
– Guaranteed for normal wash and wear

Parent Shopping Guide pdf

For a detailed step-by-step guide, please refer to the attached Parent Shopping Guide Template. This guide provides comprehensive instructions and tips to help you navigate the uniform purchasing process efficiently.

How to buy Our Uniforms Online:

  1. Visit the Uniform Shop Page:
        •  Go to the Ohio Ihsan Academy Uniform Shop: https://www.educationalapparel.com/shop-by-school/ohio-ihsan-academy/girls/
  2. Browse the Uniform Selection:
        • Explore the available uniform options categorized by gender and grade level.
        • Refer to the Ohio Ihsan Academy Student Uniform Guide to ensure you are selecting the appropriate items.
  3. Select Items:
        •  Click on the desired uniform item.
        • Choose the correct size and quantity for each item.
        • Add the selected items to your shopping cart.
  4. Review Your Cart:
        • Once you have added all necessary items to your cart, review your selections.
        • Make sure the sizes and quantities are correct.
  5. Proceed to Checkout:
        •  Click on the “Checkout” button to begin the purchase process.
  6. Provide Shipping Information:
        •  Enter the shipping details where you want the uniforms to be delivered.
        • Confirm the delivery address.
  7. Choose Payment Method:
        •  Select your preferred payment method.
        • Enter the required payment information.
  8. Place Your Order:
        • Review your order one final time.
        • Click on the “Place Order” button to complete your purchase.
  9. Order Confirmation:
        • After placing your order, you will receive an order confirmation email with the details of your purchase.
  10. In-Store Purchase Option:
        • If you prefer to buy uniforms in person, you can visit the store at the following location:

Additional Information

Order Processing Times:

– Busy season (June, July, August): Minimum 2 weeks processing before shipment.
– Slow season: 1-2 days if in stock, 1-2 weeks if backordered.
– In-store customers are prioritized over online orders.

Sizing Information:

– Sizing options for different body types.
– Slim sizes for smaller waists and slimmer legs.
– Husky and Half sizes for more room in hips or thighs, and shorter inseams.
– Junior and Teen sizes for taller/slimmer students.
– Teen Half sizes for tall and curvy students.

Return/Exchange Policy:

– Please keep tags and do not wash items until the student tries them on closer to the school year.
– Exchanges are available if needed for different sizes.
