2026–2027 School Supply List

Everything students need to prepare for the 2026–2027 school year.

Labeling Reminder

Please label all personal items with your child's name. Tissues, disinfecting wipes, and pencils are classroom supplies and do not need to be labeled.

All Students

  • School shoes (to remain at school)
  • 2 containers of Clorox or other disinfecting wipes
  • 2 boxes of facial tissues
  • Water bottle (labeled with child's name)
  • Extra change of clothes for emergencies (labeled with child's name)
  • Pack of 4 black, thin-tip Expo dry-erase markers
  • 24-count Crayola crayons
  • 12-count Crayola colored pencils
  • 2 jumbo glue sticks
  • 1 pair of blunt-tip scissors

Montessori

  • Sleeping bag for children ages 3 and 4 (labeled with child's name)
  • Small, soft comfort item for rest time (labeled with child's name)

Grades 1–5

  • Pencil box
  • 1 package of #2 pencils
  • One pair of wired USB-C headphones (labeled; no Bluetooth)
  • Prayer rug
  • Girls: Prayer garment that fully covers the arms and clothing during salah
  • Zipper pouch with handle (for storing the prayer rug and prayer garment)
