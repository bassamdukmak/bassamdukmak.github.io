# Design System: Phoenix United FC

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** Phoenix is presented as a real club first, a serious pathway second, and a football-fashion culture third. The site refuses the generic academy card grid and the previous habit of making every section shout.

**OWN WORLD.** The visual world is a club tunnel joined to an international route board: Phoenix Black and Midnight Blue hold the dense football chapters; Sandy/Cream creates editorial quiet; Phoenix Gold marks only active routes, decisions, and primary actions. Every control is square.

**STORY.** Visitors meet the club, its founders and football people, see the Radcliffe and international network proof, understand Football. Degree. Pathway., and complete a useful assessment.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** The Club opens asymmetrically with the official identity, an eight-word-or-shorter promise, real human football photography, visible application action, and a compact proof strip. Mobile reorders this deliberately rather than collapsing desktop.

**FORM.** A restrained cinematic route room: one route-board chapter, one full photographic partnership moment, one bone editorial chapter, one people ledger, one fashion product chapter, and one focused assessment.

## Brand rules

- Use only `#0D0D0D`, `#C9A84C`, `#0D1B2A`, `#E8DFC8`, and `#FFFFFF` for Phoenix-owned surfaces.
- Partner logos may retain approved external brand colours when no monochrome asset is supplied.
- Never use gradients, blends, decorative tints, coloured glow, or invented textures.
- Gold means an active route, an important decision, or a primary action. It is not ordinary decoration.
- Full crest leads official Club and matchday communication.
- Flame emblem leads PFN and lifestyle contexts.
- PHOENIX and PHOENIX UNITED wordmarks are supporting identity marks.
- The Arabic slogan is always the supplied artwork, never retyped.
- Phoenix leads co-branding. Sponsors never appear larger than Phoenix.

## Typography

- Display: Anybody Variable, compressed and uppercase, reserved for the hero, `FOOTBALL. DEGREE. PATHWAY.`, one or two chapter peaks, and the close.
- Headings: Anybody Variable, wider and quieter than the display treatment.
- Body: Public Sans Variable with a maximum readable measure near 60 characters.
- Labels: Public Sans Variable, 12px minimum, tracked only when wayfinding benefits.
- Typed Arabic: Amiri, RTL, at least 18px. The slogan itself remains artwork.
- Website display headlines stay at eight words or fewer.

## Composition

- The route concept is Dubai to Manchester to Silves, Algarve, with USA shown as a scholarship-preparation branch.
- Each section has a distinct visual job; do not repeat headline + number + image + explanation.
- Documentary photography may break the grid. Do not crop away a face, ball, player, pitch, shirt identity, or partnership context.
- Product renders use `object-fit: contain` on deliberate brand surfaces. Photoshoot images become primary editorial media when supplied.
- The founders media-day photograph is a general leadership image; no individual face labels.
- The Radcliffe photograph is partnership proof; no individual face labels.
- The partner section is a logo-only continuously rolling rail, with a static reduced-motion state.

## Motion

- One authored opening reveal and one calm partner-logo loop are enough.
- Content remains visible by default; motion enhances rather than unlocks information.
- Pause the partner rail on hover or focus. Reduced-motion users receive a static layout.

## Assessment

- Ask only the supplied qualification questions plus required consent and invisible abuse protection.
- Group the player form into About You, Football Background, What You Want, and Timing & Family.
- Match/highlight video and referral are optional. Other supplied qualification questions are required.
- Partner and kit-interest enquiries stay separate from the player assessment.
- Preserve attribution, idempotency, persistence, rate limits, queued CRM delivery, and honest outcome language.

## Launch constraints

- No launch video or video placeholder appears anywhere. A future film can be added as a later module.
- No AI-generated imagery or stock academy photography.
- No fake press stories, partner announcements, universities, products, prices beyond the supplied assessment qualifier, results, testimonials, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Joe Thompson, number 15, GoalsForCure, and related foundation content remain unpublished until written approval.
- The live website remains untouched until the private review build is approved.
