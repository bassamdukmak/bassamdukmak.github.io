OIA Iqra’ Reading Challenge

Read, Reflect, Grow!

How it works

  • The Qur’anic verse “Iqra’” (Read) marks the beginning of Quranic revelation and emphasizes the importance of seeking knowledge. At OIA, we cultivate lifelong learners who read, reflect, and grow.
  • Team Adults: teachers, staff, parents and guardians, and board members. Team Students: all OIA students.
  • Every minute of reading earns 1 point. A book recommendation video earns 10 bonus points. Total reading minutes plus bonus points determine the winner.
  • For a book recommendation, make a 1–2 minute video with the book’s title, author, suggested age range, and one reason you recommend it. Selected videos will be shared on the school’s social media pages.

How to submit

Submit reading minutes and recommendation videos daily or weekly. All minutes for each week should be submitted by Sunday night of that week.

Open Submission Form →

Important notes

  • Minutes can reflect independent or shared reading.
  • Reading may include Quran, fiction, nonfiction, and any age-appropriate texts.
  • Reading can be in any language — all languages count.
  • Honesty matters: this challenge is about building reading habits, not perfection.

The winning team

Adults: A coffee and sweets social hour at OIA.

Students: A pizza and ice cream party at the end of the month.

The winning team will be announced during the October 30 school-wide Sakinah Circle.
