Careers

As the Muslim community in Columbus continues to grow and flourish, the demand for a high-caliber Islamic education has become more pressing. Together, fueled by passion and determination, we’ve collectively contributed our time, finances, and energy to establish an innovative new Islamic school. This school is a testament to our shared commitment to meet the growing versatile needs of our community. Join us on this exhilarating journey!

We’d love to have you on our team at OIA!

Are you passionate about education and dedicated to making a positive impact in the lives of students? OIA is currently seeking talented individuals to join our team of educators and support staff.

As a OIA team member, you’ll have the opportunity to contribute to the success of our students and help shape the future of our community. Whether you’re an experienced educator, administrator, or support staff member, we welcome individuals who are committed to excellence and innovation in education.

At OIA, we value diversity, collaboration, and professional development. We offer competitive salaries, comprehensive benefits, and a supportive work environment where every team member is valued and respected.

If you’re passionate about making a difference and want to be part of a dynamic and forward-thinking school board, we encourage you to explore our current job openings and consider joining our team. Together, we can inspire and empower the next generation of leaders.

*All OIA employees and volunteers who have direct contacts with students must have an FBI & BCI background check completed before they join our team.

You can fill out the form below or send an email to hiring@oiacademy.org, and we’ll contact you as soon as possible!
