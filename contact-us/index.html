FAQ's

 

Swipe Right →

What is the admissions process?

 

The admissions process is outlined here.

Is there an entrance exam or assessment?

Yes, there is an entrance process that includes an interview for students. During the interview, we conduct informal assessments to understand each child’s abilities and learning style. These assessments help us determine if we are able to meet the student’s needs in our school and ensure that we can provide the appropriate support and resources to help every student succeed.

Do you accept EdChoice?

 

We are applying for EdChoice but cannot guarantee when it will become available. We will keep parents updated on the status and availability of EdChoice as we progress through the application process.

Which grades do you offer?

 

In our first year, Ohio Ihsan Academy will welcome Pre-K through third-grade students. We’ll then add one grade level each year. If there’s strong interest, we may also open a fourth-grade class in the first year. If you are interested in 4th grade, click here to fill the form.

What are the age requirements for the Pre-K 3 Montessori classroom?

To be eligible for our Pre-K 3 Montessori classroom, students must be 3 years old by September 30th.
