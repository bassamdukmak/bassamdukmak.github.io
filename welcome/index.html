About OIA

As the Muslim community in Columbus continues to grow and flourish, the demand for a high-caliber Islamic education has become more pressing. Together, fueled by passion and determination, we’ve collectively contributed our time, finances, and energy to establish an innovative new Islamic school. This school is a testament to our shared commitment to meet the growing versatile needs of our community. Join us on this exhilarating journey!

Our Vision

Ohio Ihsan Academy will cultivate a community of lifelong learners and compassionate leaders who are confident in their Muslim American identity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality education that integrates rigorous academic standards with rich Islamic values. We strive to cultivate a supportive learning environment that nurtures each student’s mind, heart, body, and soul. Grounded in our commitment to Ihsan, we aim to empower students to become educated, responsible, and compassionate citizens equipped to impact the world positively.

Our Philosophy

In Ohio Ihsan Academy, we will aim to foster the holistic development of our students by nurturing their hearts, bodies, minds and souls. Hearts by showing kindness and building a community. Bodies by being active and providing nutritious foods. Minds by fostering a love for learning. Last but not least, souls by fostering a deeper understanding of Allah, the Quran and their faith.

Core Values

Ohio Ihsan Academy

Nurturing Hearts, Minds, Bodies & Souls

Excellence with Purpose. Faith in Action.
