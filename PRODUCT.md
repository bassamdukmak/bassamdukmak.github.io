## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: serious footballers aged 18-25 evaluating a professional football and accredited degree pathway in Dubai, often with a parent or family member involved in the decision.
- Secondary: clubs and academies, universities and schools, recruitment partners, agents, technology partners, sponsors, and prospective employees considering a relationship with Phoenix.
- The first website release prioritizes qualified player evaluation enquiries. Institutional partnership enquiries remain available as a secondary route.

## Product Purpose

Phoenix United FC combines a UAE FA licensed competitive football environment, flexible accredited university education, and international pathways. The website must help the right prospects understand that combined offer quickly, self-qualify, and submit enough structured information for Phoenix to assess and follow up through its CRM.

Success means more qualified evaluation requests, fewer vague enquiries, clear routing between player and partnership leads, and reliable transfer of lead context into Phoenix's internal systems.

## Positioning

Phoenix brings the club, the degree, and the pathway network into one connected environment. Players are not asked to choose between serious football development and a credible academic future.

## Operating Context

- Phoenix United FC is based in Dubai and competes in the UAE football pyramid.
- Phoenix Football Network is the football education programme within the wider ecosystem.
- The current programme is designed for ages 18-25 and combines regular licensed coaching with an accredited UK or US degree studied around the football schedule.
- The network is described across three hubs: Dubai through Phoenix United FC, Manchester through Radcliffe FC, and the Algarve through Silves FC.
- Other stated routes include US college scholarships, careers and employment, camps, showcases, and youth tournaments.
- Website leads will be reviewed and worked through a CRM that is currently being completed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first release must be conversion-first, responsive, fast to understand, and ready urgently.
- It must qualify and route at least two lead types: player/pathway enquiries and partnership/general enquiries.
- Lead capture must preserve submissions even if the CRM webhook is temporarily unavailable, then be ready to forward structured data to the CRM.
- The launch evaluation accepts adult players aged 18-25 only; other age groups must be routed through a separate future flow.
- The website defines the authenticated JSON webhook contract. The CRM endpoint, bearer secret, sync secret, and internal lead-assignment rules are supplied as hosted runtime configuration.
- The supplied pathway assessment qualifies family readiness using the stated full-education-programme range of £35,000-$50,000. Do not extrapolate that qualifier into a public price list or checkout.
- Playing time, professional contracts, scholarships, and progression must never be presented as guaranteed outcomes.
- Use only verified programme, club, partner, staff, licensing, and accreditation claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Phoenix United FC name, crest, wordmark, established black/navy/cream/gold identity, and bold uppercase typographic character shown in the supplied overview and current website.
- Preserve the core language: "Football. Education. Ecosystem.", "The pitch builds character. Education builds futures. Phoenix unites them.", and "Strive to Rise / نسعى فنحيا".
- The supplied brand direction is binding, but the current website structure, page framework, and interim photography are not.
- Voice should be ambitious, direct, selective, globally minded, and honest about outcomes.

- The supplied Pathway Assessment Form is the authority for player qualification questions. It includes date of birth, nationality, residence, football level, desired route and location, readiness timing, family budget readiness, family decision-maker support, and optional referral.
- No verified testimonials, placement statistics, conversion data, commerce catalogue, or checkout terms were supplied. The site must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Qualify before collecting: the form should reveal fit and capture useful context, not merely gather contact details.
2. Prove the combined model: show football, education, and pathways as one operating system rather than three disconnected features.
3. Be selective without being vague: make standards clear while giving every serious prospect a concrete next step.
4. Route every enquiry: player and partner leads should reach the right workflow with their source and intent intact.
5. Protect trust: use verified claims, candidly state what is earned rather than guaranteed, and never let visual hype outrun the evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, clear form labels and errors, and responsive use on common mobile devices. English is the confirmed launch language; Arabic localization is an open future decision, while the Arabic brand mark remains part of the identity.
