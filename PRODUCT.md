# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Current and prospective Ohio Ihsan Academy families, students, and community members who need school information, resources, updates, forms, and clear next steps.

## Product Purpose

The website presents Ohio Ihsan Academy, helps families understand the school, and gives them direct access to admissions, student-life resources, calendars, forms, and school contacts. Success means visitors can find the correct local page or approved external school service without confusion.

## Operating Context

This is a local static redesign demo intended for later WordPress and Divi handoff. The live WordPress site is outside the authorized editing and deployment scope.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing routes, factual copy, content order, and legitimate informational destinations unless the user explicitly approves a replacement.
- Forms in the demo do not submit, authenticate, charge, upload, or save sensitive data.
- Shared navigation keeps all six links visible without a hamburger.
- School documents supplied by the user are represented as dedicated local webpages.
- Hero treatments are currently being explored on a separate selection page; they do not replace the homepage until the user chooses one.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ohio Ihsan Academy.
- Brand colors: deep green, aqua, lime, warm neutral, and white. Gold is excluded.
- Use the supplied OIA logo and authentic local school photography.
- Do not use dome or mosque-arch frames around photographs.
- Keep the tone welcoming, clear, family-centered, and school-appropriate.

## Evidence on Hand

- Local school photographs and logo assets under `wp-content/uploads/`.
- Frozen non-redesign baseline in the sibling `Original Non-Redesign` folder.
- Current combined Student Uniform Guide at `assets/redesign/images/student-uniform-guide-hd.png`.
- Local Student Handbook and School Supply List pages based on school-provided documents.

## Product Principles

- Make important family information immediately legible.
- Preserve school-provided facts and label placeholders honestly.
- Use authentic school material instead of generic stock imagery.
- Keep demo behavior safe and local.
- Let the user approve major visual changes before they replace an accepted page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic heading order, useful image alternatives, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and WCAG AA text contrast.
